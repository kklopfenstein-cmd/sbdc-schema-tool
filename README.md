 # SBDC Schema Markup Generator

A free tool from the Miami Valley Small Business Development Center that helps small business owners create JSON-LD schema markup for their websites, so search engines and AI answer engines can understand their business.

It generates connected markup for:
- Business profile (LocalBusiness and 40 specific business types)
- Services
- People (owners and key staff)
- FAQs
- Events

Live tool: https://kklopfenstein-cmd.github.io/sbdc-schema-tool/

Need help? Request free one-on-one advising from the Miami Valley SBDC: https://sbdcec.com/
