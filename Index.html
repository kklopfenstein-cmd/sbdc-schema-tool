Frequently Asked Questions

This is the single highest-value schema for AEO/GEO. Write questions the way real customers ask them, and answer in plain, complete sentences — AI answer engines lift these directly.

Rating Summary

Only use this if the numbers are real and verifiable (e.g. from Google, Yelp, or your own collected reviews). Fake or unverifiable ratings violate Google's guidelines and can get a site penalized.

Owner / Author Profile

Use this on About pages or blog posts to reinforce E-E-A-T — it tells search engines and AI systems a real, credentialed person stands behind the content.

JSON-LD Output

Paste each block inside a <script type="application/ld+json"> tag in your page's <head>. Put Business Info on every page (or at least the homepage), FAQ schema only on the page where that FAQ content actually appears, and Person schema on the relevant author/about page. Test everything with Google's Rich Results Test before publishing.
